# Cosmo Studio

Cosmo Studio is a cross-platform desktop **AI Command Center** for focused, privacy-conscious conversations with the models you already use—OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Ollama. It’s designed for teams and individuals who want a clean interface, fast switching between providers, and reliable local history without sacrificing control.

## Why Cosmo Studio

- **Unified AI workspace:** Bring multiple providers into one consistent chat experience.
- **Local-first history:** Keep conversations on your machine for quick retrieval and peace of mind.
- **Markdown-ready:** Render rich responses with code blocks and formatting out of the box.
- **Cross-platform:** Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
