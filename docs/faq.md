# FAQ

## Which providers are supported?

Cosmo Studio supports a large and growing list of providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Ollama, xAI, Groq, Mistral, Perplexity, DeepSeek, and more.

## Where is chat history stored?

By default, conversations are stored locally on your machine.

## Does Cosmo Studio upload my data?

Cosmo Studio sends prompts to the provider you configure. It does not upload your history to a separate Cosmo Studio cloud service by default.

## Can I use multiple providers?

Yes. Add as many providers as you need and switch between them per chat.
