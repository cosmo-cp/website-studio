# Cosmo Studio 1.1.6: More Model Providers

Cosmo Studio **1.1.6** adds support for Together AI, Fireworks AI, and Baseten, giving you more choices when connecting models to your workspace.

<!-- truncate -->

Configure a provider under **Settings → Providers**, then select an available model in chat. Provider credentials and usage charges may apply.
