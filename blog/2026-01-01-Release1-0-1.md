# 🚀 **Announcing Cosmo Studio 1.0.1 — Your New Cross-Platform AI Chat Client Is Here!**

We are thrilled to announce the **official release of Cosmo Studio version 1.0.1** — a sleek, powerful, and intuitive desktop AI chat application built to bring the next generation of AI interactions straight to your desktop.

Check out the project on GitHub 👉 [https://github.com/cosmo-cp/cosmo-studio](https://github.com/cosmo-cp/cosmo-studio)

---

## 🔍 What Is Cosmo Studio?

**Cosmo Studio** is a modern desktop AI client designed to make interacting with multiple AI models seamless and delightful. Whether you’re a developer, AI enthusiast, or just curious about AI assistants, Cosmo Studio puts powerful chat capabilities at your fingertips.

---

## ✨ Key Features in 1.0.1

- **🌐 Multi-Provider Support**
  Connect and switch between different AI model providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Ollama, and more — all from one app.

- **💬 Intuitive Chat Interface**
  A clean and modern UI makes conversations feel natural. Easily send prompts, explore AI responses, and engage with assistants in a distraction-free space.

- **📜 Markdown Rendering**
  Cosmo Studio renders all conversations using **Markdown**, including rich text, code blocks, links, and more — ideal for developers and writers alike.

- **🧠 Chat History Management**
  Your chats are stored locally so you can pick up right where you left off without missing a beat.

- **🖥️ Cross-Platform Compatibility**
  Works on **Windows, macOS, and Linux**, ensuring a consistent AI chat experience on your preferred platform.

- **🔜 Future Multimodal Support** _(planned)_
  The roadmap includes support for multimodal inputs such as images — take your AI experience beyond text.

---

## 🛠️ Built With Developers in Mind

Cosmo Studio is implemented using **TypeScript and modern web technologies**, providing a solid foundation that’s both extensible and approachable for contributors.

It is released under the **AGPL-3.0 license**, ensuring that the project remains open and free for the community to build upon.
